# Backtank Guard

A small client-side NeoForge 1.21.1 addon for Create, for players who keep placing their backtank by accident.

By default, right-clicking a block with a backtank in your hand places it on the ground, even when you meant to put it on. With Backtank Guard, you can only place a backtank while crouching. Without crouching, nothing is placed, so you don't drop it by mistake.
